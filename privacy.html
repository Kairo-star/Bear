SCNX Bot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Information We Collect

SCNX Bot may process basic Discord information required for its functionality, such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames, and message-related information when necessary for bot features.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide and maintain the bot's features and functionality.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share collected information with third parties except where required to operate the bot or comply with applicable law.

4. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the bot's functionality.

5. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact the bot owner through Discord.
